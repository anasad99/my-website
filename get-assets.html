Download the site assets

Your browser can reach Figma even though the build environment can't. Press the button and 25 files will download to wherever your browser puts downloads. Move them all into the assets/ folder next to index.html.

If your browser asks permission to download multiple files, allow it. If the automatic download is blocked, every filename below is also a direct link — right-click and Save As, using exactly the name shown.
